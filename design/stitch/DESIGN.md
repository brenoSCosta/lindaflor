# Linda Flor — Moda Praia

## Design System & Brand Direction

### Brand Essence

Linda Flor is a premium Brazilian swimwear brand from Aracaju. Feminine, tropical, confident — but **never kitsch**. The aesthetic sits between **MBM Swim**, **Zimmermann resort**, and **Solid & Striped**: editorial photography, generous whitespace, refined typography, and restrained use of the brand pink.

### Logo Usage (Critical)

- The logo is a **script wordmark** "Linda Flor" with a yellow hibiscus replacing the "o" in Flor, plus "Moda Praia" subline.
- **Never** place the logo inside a rectangular pink card or banner block in the UI.
- Use logo as: small centered header mark (transparent background), watermark on hero, or subtle footer — always with breathing room.
- Primary UI should rely on typography and photography, not the logo as a hero image.

### Typography

- **Display / Headlines:** High-contrast editorial serif (Cormorant Garamond / Playfair style) — large, airy, fashion-magazine feel
- **UI / Body:** Clean geometric sans (Jost / DM Sans style) — small caps for nav, 11–12px tracking on labels
- **Hierarchy:** Oversized hero headlines (72–96px desktop), thin sublines, generous line-height

### Layout Principles

- Full-bleed hero with model/lifestyle photography (warm golden hour, beach, pool)
- Asymmetric editorial grids — not symmetric card grids
- Product cards: image-first, minimal chrome, price in small caps below
- Sticky minimal header: logo center, nav left, cart/account right
- Announcement bar: thin, cream or ink — "Frete grátis acima de R$299 · Aracaju, SE"
- No heavy borders, no box shadows on everything — subtle elevation only on hover

### Photography Direction

- Warm skin tones, natural light, Brazilian coastal lifestyle
- Mix: hero full-body, detail fabric texture, flat-lay accessories
- Placeholder areas should feel like high-end fashion e-commerce, not stock clipart

### Components

- **Buttons:** Sharp corners (0–2px radius), uppercase tracked labels, pink fill or ghost outline
- **Product grid:** 2–4 columns desktop, large images, quick-add on hover
- **Filters:** Horizontal pill filters (Tamanho, Cor, Categoria) — minimal
- **Footer:** Editorial 4-column — Sobre, Ajuda, Contato, Instagram @BIQUINISLINDAFLOR

### Mood References

MBM Swim homepage, Zimmermann resort lookbooks, Solid & Striped product pages — sophisticated swimwear e-commerce, not generic Shopify template.

### Language

Portuguese (Brazil). Tone: elegante, acolhedor, verão o ano inteiro.
